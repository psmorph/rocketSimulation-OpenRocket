# Two-Stage Rocket Design and Flight Simulation using OpenRocket

This project focuses on the design and flight simulation of a two-stage model rocket using OpenRocket. It explores the fundamentals of rocket engineering, including aerodynamic stability, stage separation, motor configuration, mass distribution, and recovery systems.

The rocket is designed with a multi-stage configuration to study how sequential propulsion affects flight performance. OpenRocket is used to visualize the rocket assembly, configure flight stages, analyze stability through the center of gravity (CG) and center of pressure (CP), and estimate flight parameters such as apogee, maximum velocity, and acceleration.

## Objectives
- Design a two-stage rocket with separate booster and sustainer stages.
- Analyze aerodynamic stability and mass distribution.
- Simulate flight performance under configured motor and environmental conditions.
- Understand staging mechanics and their influence on altitude and velocity.
- Explore parachute-based recovery and simulation limitations.

## Tools Used
- **OpenRocket** — Rocket design and flight simulation.
- **Git and GitHub** — Version control and project documentation.

## Project Scope
This is an educational simulation project intended to develop practical understanding of aerospace engineering principles and multi-stage rocket design. Simulation results are predictions and may differ from real-world performance.
